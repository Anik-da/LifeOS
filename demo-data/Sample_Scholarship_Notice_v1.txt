NATIONAL MERIT SCHOLARSHIP NOTIFICATION 2026 (VERSION 1)

Issued by: Higher Education Development Board
Chairperson: Dr. Rajesh Kumar
Date: 01 October 2026

OFFICIAL NOTICE:
The Higher Education Development Board hereby invites applications for the National Merit Scholarship 2026.

1. SCHOLARSHIP AWARD:
Total Stipend: $5,000 per academic year.

2. ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Applicants must be enrolled full-time in an accredited undergraduate program.
- Minimum Cumulative GPA: 3.50.
- Must submit a valid Income Certificate verified by local Revenue Authority.

3. SUBMISSION DEADLINE:
Strict Hard Copy Submission Deadline: 15 November 2026.
Late applications will be automatically rejected.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Academic Marks Card
- Income Certificate
- Official College ID Card
