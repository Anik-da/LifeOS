NATIONAL MERIT SCHOLARSHIP NOTIFICATION 2026 (UPDATED VERSION 2)

Issued by: Higher Education Development Board
Chairperson: Dr. Rajesh Kumar
Date: 15 October 2026

UPDATED OFFICIAL NOTICE:
The Higher Education Development Board has revised the rules and deadlines for National Merit Scholarship 2026.

1. SCHOLARSHIP AWARD:
Total Stipend: $6,500 per academic year (Increased by $1,500).

2. ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Applicants must be enrolled full-time in an accredited undergraduate program.
- Minimum Cumulative GPA: 3.25 (Lowered from 3.50).
- Must submit a valid Income Certificate verified by local Revenue Authority.
- NEW REQUIREMENT: Community Service Certificate (Minimum 20 hours).

3. REVISED SUBMISSION DEADLINE:
Extended Hard Copy Submission Deadline: 01 December 2026 (Extended by 16 days).

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Academic Marks Card
- Income Certificate
- Official College ID Card
- Community Service Certificate
